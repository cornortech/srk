HOW IT WORKS 

srktask.com/how-it-works 

 

Seven Clear Steps from Enrollment to Your First Withdrawal 

This guide walks you through every step of the SRK Task process — from your first interaction with SRK University to completing your first task withdrawal. Read it in full before you begin. Understanding the process thoroughly is the single most important factor in your early success on the platform. 

 

Step 1: Join SRK University and Complete KYC 

Before you can access SRK Task, you must be an enrolled, KYC-verified member of SRK University. This is not optional. SRK Task does not have an open registration. Every person who accesses this platform has been manually verified by the SRK University enrollment team using government-issued identity documents. 

This requirement exists for a specific reason: the brands and content creators who pay for tasks on SRK Task are paying for verified, human engagement. They are not paying for bots, resellers, or anonymous accounts. The KYC requirement is the single most important trust mechanism on this platform, and it is what makes SRK Task worth more than generic open task platforms. 

The KYC process typically takes 24 to 48 hours. You will need a government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID, or driver's license), a proof-of-address document, and a clear selfie. Submit only accurate information — discrepancies are flagged automatically and will delay or prevent your verification. 

💡 Pro Tip: Use the exact name on your government ID when enrolling. Mismatched names between your ID and your enrollment details are the most common reason for KYC delays. 

 

Step 2: Link Your SRK University Account to SRK Task 

Once your SRK University verification is confirmed, linking your account to SRK Task is a one-time process that takes under five minutes. Visit srktask.com, click 'Link Account', and authenticate using your SRK University credentials. Your verified identity, profile history, and KYC status all transfer automatically. 

There is no separate SRK Task account creation. This single-credential system is intentional — it ensures that every person using SRK Task is traceable back to a verified identity. It also simplifies your experience: one login, one profile, one dashboard for both platforms. 

💡 Pro Tip: Complete all profile sections immediately after linking — including your social media platforms, preferred task categories, and a professional profile photo. A complete profile receives more task recommendations from our matching algorithm. 

 

Step 3: Explore Your Personalized Task Dashboard 

Your SRK Task dashboard is personalized to your profile from day one. Tasks are matched to your social media platforms, engagement history, skill level, and preferences. You will see tasks filtered by category, difficulty, payment range, and estimated time. 

Common task categories include social media engagement (following, liking, commenting, sharing verified brand content), content review and feedback, short written testimonials and opinions, audience surveys, brand awareness activities, and community participation. New tasks are added throughout the day. 

Do not rush. Read every task description carefully before accepting it. Check the payment amount, the specific requirements, the proof format required, and the deadline. Accept only tasks you are confident you can complete honestly and fully. 

💡 Pro Tip: High-value tasks are claimed quickly. Enable task notifications in your preferences and check your dashboard at least twice daily — especially in the morning and early evening when new tasks are most commonly published. 

 

Step 4: Complete Tasks Honestly and Thoroughly 

This step is where your earnings are built or lost. Every task on SRK Task is reviewed by AI-assisted verification and a human review team. Submission quality determines approval speed, earnings, and your profile rating — all of which directly affect your long-term earning potential on the platform. 

Complete every task exactly as the instructions specify. Do not add extra steps that weren't asked for. Do not skip steps that were. If you are asked to leave a comment on a brand's post, the comment should be genuine, relevant, and specific — not generic phrases that could apply to any post. Authentic effort is what gets approved. Shortcuts get flagged. 

The brands and creators on SRK Task are paying for real human engagement. They can tell the difference between a genuine interaction and a copy-pasted response. Treat each task as you would a small professional commitment — because that is exactly what it is. 

⚠ Important: Any use of automation tools, bots, VPNs to mask activity, or misrepresentation of completed work is detected by our AI system in real time. Violations result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of all pending earnings. There are no exceptions. 

 

Step 5: Submit Proof of Completion 

After completing a task, submit your proof as specified in the task instructions. Depending on the task type, this may include screenshots, screen recordings, links to your public social media activity, written summaries, or other documentation specified within the task. 

Good proof submissions share three qualities: they are clear and readable, they match the requirements exactly, and they are submitted promptly after completion. Blurry screenshots, incomplete submissions, and delayed proof uploads are the three most common causes of task rejections. Take an extra 60 seconds to review your submission before you click 'Submit'. 

💡 Pro Tip: Name your screenshot files descriptively before uploading (e.g., 'task-123-instagram-follow-screenshot.jpg') — this doesn't affect verification directly, but it helps you track your own submissions and respond faster if support follows up. 

 

Step 6: Earn, Track, and Withdraw 

After your proof is verified and approved, earnings are credited to your SRK Task wallet within 1 to 24 hours of verification. Verification itself typically takes 1 to 48 hours depending on the task type and the volume of submissions being reviewed. 

Track every step from your dashboard — pending verification, approved earnings, available balance, and withdrawal history. When your balance reaches the minimum withdrawal threshold, you can request a withdrawal to your bank account, digital wallet, or other available payment methods. Withdrawal requests are typically processed within 1 to 3 business days. 

💡 Pro Tip: Build your balance before your first withdrawal request. Members who withdraw small amounts frequently often wait longer relative to those who accumulate a meaningful balance first, simply because the processing cost-to-amount ratio is better. 

 

Step 7: Build Your Reputation and Unlock Premium Opportunities 

Your SRK Task profile rating is a composite score built from your task completion rate, proof quality scores, approval rate, and platform activity consistency. As your rating increases, you unlock access to premium tasks — higher-paying tasks from higher-budget brands that are reserved for members with established, trusted track records. 

The premium tier is where long-term earnings are built. Members who consistently perform at a high level across 30 to 50 tasks typically see their available task value increase by two to three times compared to their starting rate. This is not automatic — it requires consistent, quality work over time. But it is real, and it is achievable. 

Top-performing members also receive priority access to exclusive brand campaigns, higher-budget tasks, and early access to new task categories as they are rolled out. The platform rewards genuine commitment, not volume alone.

Reason 1: Blurry or Unreadable Screenshots 

This is the single most common rejection reason. Verification reviewers need to confirm specific details in your screenshots: your username, the date and time of your action, the content you engaged with, and the specific engagement action (like, comment, follow, etc.). If any of these details are obscured by a blurry photo, a cropped screenshot, or poor screen resolution, the proof cannot be verified. 

Fix: Always take screenshots directly on the device you used to complete the action. Use the native screenshot function (not a phone photo of a screen). Ensure the screenshot captures the full relevant area without cropping out identifying information. Zoom in on the task's specific requirement if necessary and include both the zoomed view and the full-page view. 

Reason 2: Missing Required Steps 

Many tasks have multiple required actions, and the proof must document all of them. For example, a task that asks you to 'like, comment, and share' a post requires proof of all three actions — not just one or two. Members who submit proof for partial completion are rejected even when they completed the full task, simply because the proof doesn't show it. 

Fix: Before starting any task, list every required action on a piece of paper or in a notes app. Check off each one as you complete it and capture proof of each. Do not submit until every item on your list has corresponding proof. 

Reason 3: Wrong Time Window 

Some tasks require actions to be completed within a specific time window — either relative to when you accept the task or on a specific date. Screenshots taken outside this window, or submissions made after the task deadline, are rejected regardless of their quality. 

Fix: Note the deadline immediately when you accept a task. Set a phone reminder 2 hours before the deadline if the task requires more than a few minutes to complete. Never accept a task you cannot complete within its stated time window. 

Reason 4: Generic Comments or Engagement That Violates Task Requirements 

Tasks that require written engagement — comments, reviews, or feedback — are reviewed for authenticity by both the AI system and human reviewers. Generic phrases that could apply to any piece of content ('Great post!' / 'Loved this!') are flagged as inauthentic engagement, even when they technically fulfill the letter of the task requirement. 

Fix: Read the content you are engaging with before you engage with it. Your comment should reference something specific from the post — a point it made, a question it raised, or a reaction specific to the content. Authentic, specific engagement is what the brands paying for these tasks are paying for. 

Reason 5: Automation Detected 

SRK Task's AI monitoring system analyzes behavioral patterns in real time. Actions completed at inhuman speed, multiple actions from the same IP address across different accounts, and engagement patterns that do not match organic human behavior are all flagged as potential automation. This applies even if you did not use any tools — the AI flags anything that looks anomalous. 

Fix: Complete tasks at a natural pace. Don't rush through the engagement actions. Take the actions genuinely, give the content your real attention, and submit proof after a realistic engagement window. This is not just about avoiding detection — it is about the quality of engagement you provide. 

Conclusion 

Almost every rejection on SRK Task is preventable. The common thread across all rejection reasons is the same: rushing. Take more time with each step, and your approval rate will reflect it. A 95%+ approval rate is achievable for any member who treats task completion as careful, professional work.

Every task submission contributes to your approval rate. A careless submission on a low-value task damages your rating just as much as a careless submission on a high-value one. Apply the same proof discipline to every submission, regardless of the payment amount.

High-value tasks fill within hours of posting. Members who check their dashboard once or twice a week consistently miss the best opportunities. Enable push notifications for new task matches and check your dashboard at least twice daily. This single habit has a measurable positive impact on earnings. 

Mistake 5: Withdrawing Earnings Immediately in Small Amounts 

Many payment methods have minimum thresholds and processing costs that make very small withdrawals inefficient. Building your balance before withdrawing reduces the relative impact of any transaction costs and ensures your withdrawal processing time is used efficiently.
